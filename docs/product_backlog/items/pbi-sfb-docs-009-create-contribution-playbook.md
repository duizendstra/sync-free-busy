## 1. PBI Goal & Justification
**Goal:** To create a clear, actionable feedback loop for the continuous improvement of our own framework.
**Justification:** A system without a feedback mechanism cannot evolve. This playbook makes our core principle of "Learn Fast" an actionable process for every team member.
