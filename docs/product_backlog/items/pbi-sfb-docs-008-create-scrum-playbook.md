## 1. PBI Goal & Justification
**Goal:** To formalize our agile way of working, ensuring all team members understand their roles and the process.
**Justification:** A documented process is repeatable, consistent, and easier to improve. This playbook is critical for onboarding and for ensuring the team operates efficiently.
